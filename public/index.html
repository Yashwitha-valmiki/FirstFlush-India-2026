METHODOLOGY

How the estimate works

The model combines dry-period duration, rainfall, rainfall intensity, paved-surface exposure, traffic, construction, waste, blockage, and water-body proximity. The result is a relative prioritization estimate. It does not measure pollutant concentration or replace professional field judgment.
